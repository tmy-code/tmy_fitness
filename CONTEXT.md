# TMY Fitness

A personal, self-hosted training companion for following Olivier Lafay's Access Autonomy method (Méthode Lafay 2A) on a phone, offline, with the owner's data kept on their own server.

## Language

The app speaks French throughout; the method's own words are used exactly as the book uses them.

### La méthode

**Méthode Lafay 2A**:
The bodyweight training method described in Olivier Lafay's book *Access Autonomy*; the program this app follows.
_Avoid_: Access Autonomy (that is the book), programme Lafay

**Tests**:
The initial assessment, in steps for each area (Test poitrine, Test dos, Test cuisses, possibly on different days), whose Résultats decide the starting Niveau: Découverte, Niveau 1 or Niveau 2. They are recorded in the Carnet as the first entries.

**Niveau**:
A stage of the method (Découverte, then 1 to 20, plus the bis Niveaux) made of Blocs, with its own recommended Fréquence.
_Avoid_: Level, palier

**Niveau bis**:
An optional intermediate Niveau (2-bis, 3-bis, 4-bis) for those who need an easier step between Niveau 1 and 2.

**Bloc**:
A fixed group of exercises for one body area within a Niveau (Bloc Poitrine, Bloc Dos, Bloc Cuisses, Bloc Taille…), separated from the next Bloc by a longer rest.
_Avoid_: Module (a module is a user-varied grouping, not part of the prescribed Niveau)

**Séance**:
One of the prescribed workouts a Niveau alternates between (Séance 1, Séance 2), from Niveau 7 onward; the book's label is kept as-is.
_Avoid_: using Séance for a workout actually performed (that is an Entraînement)

**Programme**:
One of the successive prescribed workouts of an early Niveau (Premier programme, then Deuxième programme once its target is reached).
_Avoid_: Programme for the method as a whole

**Exercice**:
A specific movement identified by its code — a letter, optionally followed by a number (A1, B3, E6, or B alone); progress is tracked per Exercice. It has a short name, the book page, and an optional description the owner writes in their own words (kept only in the private Méthode).
_Avoid_: Variante, exercise, mouvement

**Famille**:
All the Exercices sharing a letter (Famille A, Famille B); the book's "Exercice A" chapter describes a Famille.
_Avoid_: Exercice A (when meaning the whole group)

**Exercices additionnels**:
Optional extra exercises a Niveau offers for a specific area (bras, cuisses, taille, avant-bras, cou, mollets).

**Échauffement**:
The warm-up done before every Entraînement (quick stretches, a few minutes of cardio, two light Séries of A).

**Split**:
Spreading one Séance or Programme over consecutive days by doing only some of its Blocs each day.

**Routine de souplesse**:
A sequence of stretches, each held for a time, from the book's Souplesse chapter; done on its own schedule, separate from Entraînements, and recorded only as done.
_Avoid_: Étirements, stretching session

### Exécution

**Entraînement**:
One workout actually performed on a given day, following all of one Séance or Programme of the current Niveau, or only some of its Blocs when using a Split; it is what the Carnet records.
_Avoid_: Séance (in the sense of "ma séance d'hier"), workout, session

**Série**:
One uninterrupted run of an exercise, ended by reaching the target or the Échec.
_Avoid_: Set

**Consigne**:
The instruction for one Exercice within a Séance or Programme: its Séries with a target for each (a number of repetitions, max, or a duration), the rythme, the Temps de repos, plus a free-text note for anything unusual.
_Avoid_: Prescription, instruction

**Résultat**:
What one Série actually achieved: a number of répétitions, or seconds held for a timed Exercice.

**Répétitions forcées**:
Extra répétitions squeezed out past the Échec (with help or a simpler Exercice), recorded apart from the Résultat ("12 + 3").

**Échec**:
The point where not one more correct repetition is possible; "max" means going to the Échec.

**Temps de repos**:
The prescribed rest between Séries, between exercises, or between Blocs.

**Fréquence**:
How often the trainee trains; each Niveau recommends one, and the trainee may adjust it.

### Progression

**Progression**:
The ordered list of Exercices the book leads through for one Consigne (C7 → C1 → C3), each with the condition for passing to the next; the trainee decides when to pass.
_Avoid_: Échelle, ladder

**Objectif du jour**:
The target for each Série of today's Entraînement: the last Résultat plus the Consigne's increment (usually +1), or the Consigne's starting number the first time; for max Séries, the last Résultat to beat.
_Avoid_: Target, goal

**Exercice actuel**:
The Exercice the trainee currently performs for a Consigne — a step of its Progression, or a substitute they chose; kept until they change it.

**Remplacement**:
Doing a different Exercice than the Consigne names, outside its Progression (F replaced by E4 for knee pain); the Résultats belong to the Exercice actually done.
_Avoid_: Substitution, swap

**Changement de niveau**:
Moving on to the next Niveau; decided by the trainee when progress stalls or lassitude sets in, not by a fixed rule.

**Boucle**:
Restarting a Niveau (the current one or an earlier one) from the beginning with Objectifs well below one's best (30–40% lower), to break a plateau or consolidate foundations.
_Avoid_: Loop, reset

**Mini-boucle**:
Dropping the Objectif du jour of a single stalled Exercice to a lower starting number and climbing up again from there, while the rest of the Niveau continues normally.

**Boucle augmentée**:
A Niveau built as a structured review of earlier Niveaux (Niveau 11 and Niveau 20).

**Parcours**:
The timeline of Niveaux the trainee has gone through, including Boucles (Niveau 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → Boucle 2 → 3…).
_Avoid_: Path, historique des niveaux

**Carnet**:
The record of every Série performed — exercise and repetitions — used to see progress and decide on Boucles and Changements de niveau.
_Avoid_: Journal, log, historique
